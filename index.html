Who we are

Rooted in Panjabari, working across Assam

FSD Trust was founded under the leadership of social workers Sri Jayanta Borah and Sri Dipak Kakaty, with community development through social work as its purpose. At a general meeting in Panjabari, Guwahati, members formed an Executive Committee and registered the organisation under the Societies Registration Act XXI of 1860.

Since then the Trust has run vocational training, health programmes, environment and income generation work, cultural programmes and women's empowerment initiatives, with support from government departments, banks, foundations and research institutions.

Our work is concentrated in Kamrup (Metro), Morigaon and Biswanath, with water, environment and livelihood projects in Kokrajhar, Chirang, Dhubri, Goalpara, Bongaigaon and Lakhimpur.

Vision

A close and caring view of the marginalised, poor and needy people of every community in Assam who are socially and economically backward, so that they gain lasting benefits.

Mission

To move steadily towards ending poverty and to uplift the poorest and most marginalised people of society through health, education, livelihood, skill development, environment and related services.

Participation

A participatory approach to sustainable development.

Empowerment

Women, children, older people, ST, SC, OBC and minority communities.

Independence

Economic independence as the key to development.

Local resources

Making the best use of resources communities already have.

What we do

Six areas of work

Drawn from the objectives in our Memorandum of Association and the programmes we have delivered.

Health & HIV prevention

HIV Targeted Intervention from Jorabat with Assam State AIDS Control Society, a 15-bed urban homeless shelter in Morigaon, mobile health camps and care for older people.

Handloom, jute & crafts

Jacquard handloom training for weavers under the SAMARTH scheme, jute bag and jute craft enterprise programmes, hand embroidery and detergent making.

Farm & fisheries livelihoods

Fish farmer livelihoods in Morigaon, piggery producer organisations in Boko, dairy cooperatives and Joint Liability Groups with NABARD.

Water & sanitation

Completed work as Implementation Support Agency under Jal Jeevan Mission in five PHE divisions, and household latrine construction with geotagging under Swachh Bharat Mission (Urban).

Environment & herbal gardens

Restoration of degraded land with TERI under the GEF Small Grants Programme (OP-7 completed, OP-8 coming soon), herbal gardens in schools with the National Medicinal Plants Board, and Earth Day outreach.

Skills, education & safety

Placement-linked skill training, pre-vocational training for children in RSTC/NRST centres, entrepreneurship programmes and fire safety drills in schools.
